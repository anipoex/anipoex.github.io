hero:
  name: Anipoex
  text: Every source, one tracker
  tagline: An AniList manga tracker that compares every source for each manga and syncs privately across iOS, Android, macOS and Windows.
  actions:
    - theme: brand
      text: Get started
      link: https://github.com/anipoex/anipoex#-download
    - theme: alt
      text: View on GitHub
      link: https://github.com/anipoex/anipoex

features:
  - icon: 🔀
    title: Multi-source scans
    details: Every source per manga compared, with +N for each. Outdated and failing ones step aside, and the furthest ahead is picked for you, with Undo.
  - icon: 🔐
    title: Private sync
    details: Picks, scans, settings and extensions follow you through your own GitHub gist or synced folder. No account, no server.
  - icon: 📋
    title: AniList is the library
    details: Status, progress, score and dates are read from and saved to your tracker directly. No second library to keep in step.
  - icon: 🧩
    title: Mihon extensions everywhere
    details: The same extensions run natively on Android and in an embedded JVM on iOS and the desktop app.
  - icon: 📖
    title: Reader
    details: Chapter pages from the chosen source, with your progress saved back to AniList.
  - icon: 📱
    title: Native on each platform
    details: SwiftUI on iOS, Jetpack Compose on Android, and desktop apps for macOS and Windows.
